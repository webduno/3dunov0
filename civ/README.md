# 🏛️ Civilization AR - 3D Face Tracking Game

A revolutionary augmented reality civilization-building game that uses facial tracking and voice commands to create an immersive 3D city-building experience.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Getting Started
1. **Allow Camera Access**: When prompted, allow the game to access your camera for face tracking
2. **Allow Microphone Access**: Enable microphone access for voice commands
3. **Say "START"** or click the "Start Civilization" button to begin
4. **Look around** by turning your head to control the 3D view
5. **Use voice commands** to build your civilization

### 🎯 Core Gameplay

#### Face Tracking Controls
- **Head Movement**: Turn your head left/right and up/down to look around the 3D terrain
- **Eye Tracking**: Your eyes are tracked with red laser beams showing your targeting direction
- **Nose Tracking**: A yellow dot shows your center point for precise placement

#### Voice Commands

##### 🏗️ Building Commands
Build different structures by saying:
- **"House"** - Basic housing (costs 10, adds 5 population)
- **"Farm"** - Food production (costs 15, adds 10 food)
- **"Mine"** - Gold production (costs 20, adds 15 gold)
- **"Temple"** - Culture generation (costs 30, adds 20 culture)
- **"Barracks"** - Military training (costs 25, adds 10 military)
- **"Library"** - Science research (costs 35, adds 15 science)
- **"Market"** - Trade center (costs 40, adds 25 trade)
- **"Palace"** - Royal residence (costs 100, adds 50 prestige)

##### 👥 Unit Commands
Create and control units by saying:
- **"Worker"** - Basic laborer (costs 5, adds 2 population)
- **"Warrior"** - Military unit (costs 10, adds 2 population)
- **"Archer"** - Ranged unit (costs 15, adds 2 population)
- **"Settler"** - Expansion unit (costs 20, adds 2 population)

### 📊 Game Statistics

#### Primary Metrics
- **Population**: Your civilization's total population
- **Happiness**: Remaining lives (starts at 3)
- **Culture**: Cultural achievements and bonuses
- **Era**: Current game level

The game features dynamic missions that provide objectives and rewards:
- **Master Builder**: Build 10 houses to establish your city
